Welcome

The Old Age Pension Scheme provides a monthly pension of Rs. 250 to eligible senior citizens aged 60 years or older who are residents of Purvanchal.

You do not need to pay anyone to apply. If you are applying for the first time, a government help desk at your Block Development Office will assist you free of charge. Ask for a receipt and keep your application number.

How to apply

  1. Enter your mobile number and the one-time code sent to you.
  2. Enter your name exactly as it appears on your age proof.
  3. Enter your address and bank account details. You can correct a pending application.
  4. Upload an age proof document (JPG or PDF, maximum 5 MB).
  5. Read the final page, submit, and save or print the acknowledgment number.

Never share your OTP or bank PIN. The department will never charge a fee to submit this application.
